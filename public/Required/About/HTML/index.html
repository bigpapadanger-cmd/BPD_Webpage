BPD GAMING NETWORK

About Us

BPD Gaming Network is a collaboratively operated gaming community and development project focused on helping players connect, compete, organize matches, and access useful gaming tools and resources.

Our primary development focus is currently Rocket League, including player profiles, matchmaking, private matches, seasonal competition, match scheduling, and scoreboard result verification.

BPD Gaming Network also provides access to other gaming content and resources, including ARK: Survival Ascended and Minecraft mod projects that may link to external distribution platforms.

The platform remains under active development. Features, supported games, community tools, account systems, and competitive services may continue to expand and change as development progresses.

BPD Gaming Network is independently operated and is not endorsed by, sponsored by, or affiliated with Epic Games, Psyonix, Microsoft, Mojang, the owners of ARK: Survival Ascended, CurseForge, or other third-party game publishers or platforms unless expressly stated.

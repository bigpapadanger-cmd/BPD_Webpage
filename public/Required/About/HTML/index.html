BPD GAMING NETWORK

About Us

BPD Gaming Network is a community-focused gaming hub built to make it easier for players to connect, compete, and keep track of their matches.

Our current focus is Rocket League, with tools for weekly matchmaking, private matches, player connections, and scoreboard result submissions. As the community grows, we plan to expand into additional games and resources.

BPD Gaming Network is independently operated and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Epic Games, Psyonix, or Rocket League.

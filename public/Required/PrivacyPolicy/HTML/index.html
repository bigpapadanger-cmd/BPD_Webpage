Users must be at least 18 years old or otherwise meet the legal age requirements applicable in their country or region of residence to use account-based and interactive features without parental or legal-guardian consent.

If a user does not meet the applicable age requirement, permission from a parent or legal guardian is required before registering for or using those features.

By signing in, registering, and making that confirmation, the user represents that the information provided is accurate and that any required parental or legal-guardian consent has been obtained.

2. Information We Collect

Epic Games account information

When you choose to authenticate with Epic Games, we may receive limited Epic account information such as an account identifier, display name, or preferred username.

This information is used to authenticate users, help personalize user pages, associate activity with the correct account, reduce fake or duplicate accounts, and help verify that users participating in matches are authentic.

BPD Gaming Network does not receive or store your Epic Games password.

BPD Gaming Network profile information

Users may provide profile information such as a display name, contact information, Rocket League rank, preferred game mode, availability, notification preferences, timezone, regional information, and online-status preferences.

This information may be used internally to create matches, organize seasons, improve matchmaking, notify users about matches, personalize account features, and operate other community functions.

Contact information

Depending on the features you choose to use, we may collect an email address, telephone number, or both for account-related communications and match notifications.

Location and regional information

Where available, our infrastructure may determine an approximate location from network information such as an IP address. This may include country, state, region, timezone, or similar approximate geographic information.

Regional information may be used to help determine player regions and support more appropriate or fair matchmaking, season organization, and match scheduling.

Technical and security information

Our systems and service providers may process technical information including IP addresses, browser or device information, timestamps, requested pages, referral information, authentication cookies, security events, service errors, and diagnostic logs.

This information may be used to operate the website, prevent abuse, investigate failures, protect accounts, troubleshoot technical problems, and maintain service security.

4. Rocket League Scoreboard Images and OCR Processing

BPD Gaming Network allows users to submit Rocket League match-result screenshots for scoreboard processing. These images are primarily processed to identify and verify numerical match statistics.

Player names appearing on a submitted scoreboard may be read as part of the verification process. Their primary purpose in this process is to help confirm that the scoreboard corresponds to the expected active match and expected participants.

The OCR and image-matching systems are primarily focused on numerical statistics rather than building recognition models based on player names or other alphabetical identifiers.

Where possible, scoreboard images may be cropped so that processing and retained images focus primarily on the scoreboard and relevant match information rather than unrelated portions of the original screenshot.

5. Scoreboard Retention and Recognition Training

Scoreboard images may be retained to support match-result verification, investigate OCR errors, maintain accurate season records, and improve scoreboard-reading reliability.

Images associated with a season are intended to be kept only for the period reasonably necessary to support that season and generally no longer than 30 days after the applicable season has ended.

For example, if a season lasts approximately three months, associated scoreboard images may remain available during that season and for up to approximately 30 additional days afterward.

A limited amount of image-derived data may be retained for improving numerical recognition. This training data is intended to focus on finite examples of numerical values and scoreboard statistics used to improve number matching and recognition.

The purpose of this training process is not to develop a database for recognizing player names or other alphabetical personal identifiers.

Users should not intentionally submit unrelated personal photographs, private communications, financial information, government identifiers, or other sensitive information as part of a scoreboard submission.

6. Account and Profile Data Retention

Account and profile information may be retained while the user's BPD Gaming Network account remains active.

Account information may be removed when the user requests deletion of the account or when BPD Gaming Network administrators remove or terminate the account.

BPD Gaming Network is still under development, and a self-service account-deletion feature is not currently available. Account-management and deletion functionality will be expanded as the account system is completed.

Some information may need to remain temporarily in backups, security records, match records, or other operational systems after an account is removed where reasonably necessary for security, integrity, dispute resolution, legal obligations, or technical backup processes.

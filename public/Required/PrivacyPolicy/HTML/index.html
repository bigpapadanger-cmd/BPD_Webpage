Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: August 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BPD Gaming Network collects, uses, shares, retains, and protects information when you visit our website or use our gaming, account, community, match, and scoreboard services.

1. Age Requirements and Parental Permission

BPD Gaming Network is not intended for children under 13, and children under 13 may not create an account or use interactive features. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Users ages 13 through 17 may use the service only with permission and supervision from a parent or legal guardian. A parent or guardian must authorize any purchase made by a minor and must approve the payment method used.

If we learn that information was collected from a child under 13, we may suspend the account and delete the information. A parent or guardian may contact us at privacy@bpd-gaming-network.com to request review or deletion.

2. Information We Collect

Epic Games account information

When you choose to log in with Epic Games, Epic may provide a limited account identifier and display name needed to authenticate you and connect your BPD Gaming Network account. We do not receive or store your Epic Games password.

Account and profile information

We may collect information you submit, including gamer names, platform, rank, team information, contact details, communication preferences, match availability, profile settings, and parental-permission confirmations.

Scoreboard and OCR information

When you submit a Rocket League scoreboard, we receive the cropped image area, expected player names, match size, extracted scoreboard statistics, confidence values, validation results, warnings, job identifiers, processing time, and diagnostic information needed to operate and improve the OCR system.

Communications

We may collect messages, support requests, reports, feedback, and other information you send to us.

Technical and usage information

Our systems and service providers may automatically process IP addresses, browser and device information, timestamps, requested pages, referral information, cookie identifiers, security events, error logs, and approximate location derived from an IP address.

Payment information

If paid features are introduced, payment information may be processed by a third-party payment provider. BPD Gaming Network does not intend to directly store complete payment-card numbers or card security codes. The payment provider's own privacy policy will also apply.

3. How We Use Information

  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Create and administer accounts, profiles, teams, matches, and community features.
  • Process scoreboard images and generate OCR reports.
  • Validate results, investigate errors, prevent duplicate jobs, and improve reliability.
  • Respond to support requests, reports, and user communications.
  • Protect users, enforce rules, prevent abuse, and maintain service security.
  • Provide, measure, maintain, and improve the website and its features.
  • Display advertising and measure advertising performance where permitted.
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect legal rights.

4. Scoreboard Images and OCR Processing

Scoreboard images may contain player display names and gameplay statistics. Images are transmitted to our OCR infrastructure for processing. Images, temporary processing files, extracted results, and debugging information may be retained for only as long as reasonably needed to complete the job, diagnose failures, secure the service, and improve report reliability.

Do not upload unrelated photographs, private communications, government identifiers, financial information, or images containing sensitive personal information. You must have the right to submit the image.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use essential cookies to maintain authentication, protect the Epic login flow, remember settings, and provide security. These cookies are necessary for requested account features.

Advertising, analytics, and embedded services may use cookies, local storage, pixels, or similar technologies subject to consent requirements and available controls. Browser settings may allow you to block or delete cookies, but disabling essential cookies can prevent login and other features from working.

6. Google AdSense and Advertising

We may use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process device information, IP addresses, cookie identifiers, advertising interactions, and activity information to deliver, limit, measure, and protect advertisements, subject to applicable consent choices and Google's policies.

Where required, we will request consent before using non-essential advertising technologies. Google provides information and controls at Google Advertising Policies and My Ad Center.

We do not intend to use personalized advertising based on users known by us to be under 13. Users under 13 are not permitted to create accounts or use interactive features.

7. Service Providers and Data Sharing

We may share information with service providers only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, support, and improve the service. Current or anticipated providers include:

  • Cloudflare for website delivery, Worker execution, security, DNS, and related infrastructure.
  • Epic Games for account authentication and Epic account services.
  • Google Cloud for OCR processing and application infrastructure.
  • Google Sheets and Apps Script for authorized operational records, testing metrics, and result tracking.
  • Google AdSense for advertising delivery and measurement.
  • Payment providers if paid features are introduced.

We may also disclose information when required by law, to investigate fraud or abuse, to protect safety or legal rights, or as part of a merger, reorganization, financing, or transfer of the service. We do not sell personal information for money.

8. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including maintaining accounts, providing requested services, resolving disputes, preventing abuse, meeting legal obligations, and improving system reliability. Retention periods vary by information type and feature.

Account information may remain until the account is deleted or becomes inactive under our retention practices. Security, transaction, and legal records may be retained longer when reasonably necessary. Backup copies may persist for a limited period before being overwritten.

9. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection regarding personal information, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may also:

  • Sign out to end the active browser session.
  • Adjust browser cookie controls and advertising preferences.
  • Request account or personal-information deletion.
  • Ask what categories of personal information we maintain about you.
  • Appeal a denied privacy request where applicable law provides that right.

Submit requests to privacy@bpd-gaming-network.com. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling a request. Authorized agents and parents or guardians acting for eligible minors may be required to provide proof of authority.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted connections, restricted secrets, authenticated service calls, and signed session cookies. No internet service or storage system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We will update this Policy when material practices change and revise the date displayed above. Development status does not reduce the privacy rights available under applicable law.
